## Why AI-Generated Location Pages Get Flagged as Thin Content and How to Avoid the Penalty

Thin location pages destroy your garage door SEO faster than having no page at all. Here is how to build location pages for your garage door business that survive scrutiny from every search engine and AI answer engine:

1. **Add a real service radius table.** List response times by zip code - for example, "Naperville: 45-minute average dispatch" - so Google AI Overviews and Perplexity extract a concrete, verifiable fact.
2. **Name the technician or team lead for that city.** A named expert tied to a specific market signals E-E-A-T to Google and entity authority to ChatGPT and Gemini.
3. **Include one local-specific repair detail.** Reference the dominant garage door model in that area, a common weather-related repair issue, or a local building code that affects installation.
4. **Embed third-party review excerpts.** Perplexity and ChatGPT surface businesses with strong off-page mentions; a quoted review from a named homeowner in that city reinforces both signals.
5. **Add schema markup for the service area and business entity.** Google AI Overviews still rewards structured data that confirms your garage door SEO service covers that specific geography.

Sites that try to rescue individual thin pages rarely recover. The proven recovery pattern is to consolidate thin content into comprehensive city guides, redirect the old URLs, and rebuild authority around a smaller number of genuinely useful resources.<sup><a href="#sources-cited-3" id="ref-cit_005-1">[3]</a></sup> For SEO for garage door companies, that means fewer pages done right - not more pages done fast.
